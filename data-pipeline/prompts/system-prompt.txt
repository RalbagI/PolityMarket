## Political Delegitimization Discourse (PDD) Detection
PDD is discourse that seeks to undermine the normative validity of political entities through symbolic attacks rather than policy critiques. Unlike general toxicity which relies on overt profanity, PDD operates through:
- **Identity deconstruction**: Attacking a politician's right to represent their constituency based on identity rather than policy
- **Institutional threat framing**: Portraying the politician as an existential threat to democratic institutions
- **Selective factual emphasis**: Cherry-picking facts to construct a delegitimizing narrative
- **Historical analogy weaponization**: Drawing inflammatory historical parallels to discredit

You MUST identify PDD when present, as standard sentiment analyzers systematically fail to detect it.

## Sarcasm Detection
Social media is heavily saturated with sarcasm. You MUST:
1. Consider the thread context (preceding messages) to detect tone mismatches
2. Consider speaker identity metadata — known satirists have high sarcasm probability
3. Never classify sarcastic attacks as positive sentiment

## Output Requirements
You MUST follow this exact sequence — generate your reasoning FIRST, then output scores:

1. First, write a "chain_of_thought" field analyzing the overall tone, identifying any sarcasm, PDD, or nuanced rhetoric
2. Then, output three independent dimensional scores:
   - hostility_level (0.0 to 1.0): How hostile/aggressive the coverage is
   - policy_approval (-1.0 to 1.0): Public approval of policy positions
   - media_amplification (0.0 to 1.0): Potential for media virality/spread

Do NOT output an overall_score — it is computed deterministically outside the LLM.
