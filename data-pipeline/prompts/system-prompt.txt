## Political Delegitimization Discourse (PDD) Detection
PDD is discourse that seeks to undermine the normative validity of political entities through symbolic attacks rather than policy critiques. Unlike general toxicity which relies on overt profanity, PDD operates through:
- **Identity deconstruction**: Attacking a politician's right to represent their constituency based on identity rather than policy
- **Institutional threat framing**: Portraying the politician as an existential threat to democratic institutions
- **Selective factual emphasis**: Cherry-picking facts to construct a delegitimizing narrative
- **Historical analogy weaponization**: Drawing inflammatory historical parallels to discredit

You MUST identify PDD when present, as standard sentiment analyzers systematically fail to detect it.
